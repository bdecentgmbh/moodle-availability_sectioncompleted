# Restriction by other section completion — Student guide

Some activities and sections of a course open only after you have completed the activities of another
section. You then see why, for example:

*Not available unless: Week 1 completed. (2 of 3 so far)*

## Reading the message

- **"Week 1 completed"** — complete all activities of *Week 1* that have completion tracking, including
  those in its subsections.
- **"At least 2 of the activities in Week 1 completed"** — any 2 of them are enough.
- **"completed and passed"** — the activities need a passing grade. Completing them without passing
  is not enough.
- **"completed and failed"** — the activities need a failing grade. Teachers use this to offer extra
  help after a failed quiz.
- **"(2 of 3 so far)"** — how many of the activities you have already completed in the way required.

## Completing the activities

Open the section the message names and look at each activity's completion conditions, such as
*View*, *Receive a grade* or a **Mark as done** button. When you complete the last activity you need,
the course page reloads and the activity or section opens.

If the message doesn't change after you completed everything, ask your teacher: an activity of the
section may be hidden from you.
